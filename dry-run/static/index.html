1. Interaction capture area (manual, optional)

Your mouse movements, keystrokes in the field below, scrolling in this box, clicks, tab switches, and pastes are all being recorded client-side. Nothing is sent anywhere until you click a button.

Scroll inside this box a little -- scroll behavior (distance, direction reversals, timing) is one of the signals the detector extracts.

The library extracts several feature groups: mouse dynamics (path efficiency, velocity/acceleration/jerk), keystroke dynamics (dwell/flight time), scroll behavior, click position variance, and tab-focus / paste behavior.

A scripted bot's mouse path tends to be a near-straight line at constant velocity; a real human's path is curvier and speed varies. Scripted keystrokes tend to land at uniform, often suspiciously short intervals; real typing rhythm varies and includes hesitations.

Try switching to another browser tab and back -- that's a focus/blur event, another signal the detector uses.
